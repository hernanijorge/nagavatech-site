Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information we collect

When you contact us, request a quote, or place an order, we collect the information you provide directly: name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, shipping address (for hardware orders) and any message content you send us.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, process orders, provide support for apps and hardware you've purchased, and send order or shipping updates. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payments

Purchases are processed through third-party payment providers (such as Stripe or PayPal). We do not store your full card details on our servers — those are handled directly by the payment provider under their own privacy policy.

4. Communication

If you reach out by email or WhatsApp, we use that information solely to respond to you and, where relevant, follow up about your order or request. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

5. Data retention

We keep contact and order information for as long as needed to provide support and comply with tax and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing nagavatech@gmail.com.

7. Cookies & analytics

This site does not use third-party advertising cookies. Basic, privacy-respecting analytics may be used to understand overall traffic; no personally identifying data is sold or shared as a result.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to nagavatech@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +1 (339) 600-8241.
